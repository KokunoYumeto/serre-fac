\phantomsection\label{fac:II:1:no:33}
\textbf{33. Products.}
If $r$ and $r'$ are two integers $\geq 0$, we identify the affine space $K^{r+r'}$ with the product $K^r\times K^{r'}$. The Zariski topology of $K^{r+r'}$ is \textit{finer} than the product of the Zariski topologies of $K^r$ and $K^{r'}$; it is in fact strictly finer if $r$ and $r'$ are both positive. It follows that, if $U$ and $U'$ are locally closed subspaces of $K^r$ and $K^{r'}$, then $U\times U'$ is a locally closed subspace of $K^{r+r'}$, and the sheaf $\mathcal{O}_{U\times U'}$ is well defined.

Let $W$ be a locally closed subspace of $K^t$, where $t\geq0$, and let $\varphi:W\to U$ and $\varphi':W\to U'$ be two maps. \hyperref[fac:II:1:no:32:prop:5]{Proposition~5} immediately gives:

\phantomsection\label{fac:II:1:no:33:prop:6}
\textsc{Proposition 6.} \textit{The map $x\mapsto(\varphi(x),\varphi'(x))$ from $W$ to $U\times U'$ is regular if and only if $\varphi$ and $\varphi'$ are regular.}

Since every \textit{constant} map is regular, the \hyperref[fac:II:1:no:33:prop:6]{preceding proposition} shows that every \textit{section} $x\mapsto(x,x'_0)$, with $x'_0\in U'$, is a regular map from $U$ to $U\times U'$. Moreover, the \textit{projections} $U\times U'\to U$ and $U\times U'\to U'$ are plainly regular.

Let $V$ and $V'$ be locally closed subspaces of $K^s$ and $K^{s'}$, and let $\psi:U\to V$ and $\psi':U'\to V'$ be two maps. The preceding remarks, together with \hyperref[fac:II:1:no:33:prop:6]{Proposition~6}, give the following (cf. [1], Chapter~IV):

\phantomsection\label{fac:II:1:no:33:prop:7}
\textsc{Proposition 7.} \textit{The map $\psi\times\psi':U\times U'\to V\times V'$ is regular if and only if $\psi$ and $\psi'$ are regular.}

Hence:

\phantomsection\label{fac:II:1:no:33:cor:prop:7}
\textsc{Corollary.} \textit{The map $\psi\times\psi'$ is a biregular isomorphism if and only if $\psi$ and $\psi'$ are biregular isomorphisms.}
